Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026 · This is a demo document, not legal advice.

Our commitment

TUDO is built privacy-first. We collect the minimum needed to run a safe dating service, we never sell your personal data, and you stay in control of what others see.

What we collect

  • Account details you provide: name, date of birth, email, mobile, and password (stored hashed).
  • Profile content: photos, bio, interests, preferences and prompts you choose to add.
  • Approximate location — never your exact home address.
  • Usage and device information to keep the service secure and reliable.

What we never expose publicly

  • Your exact residential address.
  • Your private email address or mobile number.
  • Identity or verification documents — these are access-controlled and never shown to other members.

How we use your information

To create your profile, surface compatible people, enable private messaging between matched users, provide safety and verification features, process subscriptions through certified payment providers, and comply with the law.

Your choices & rights

  • Control who can see your photos, online status and approximate location.
  • Use Incognito Mode, pause discovery, or deactivate temporarily.
  • Download a copy of your data, or delete your account permanently at any time.
  • Manage marketing consent and notifications in Settings.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep TUDO secure and functional. With your consent, we also use analytics cookies to improve matching and the overall experience. You can choose Accept All, Essential Only, or manage preferences from the banner. Non-essential tracking is never enabled before you consent.

Security

Access to personal data is restricted with authentication and, in production, database row-level security so members can only reach their own data and the conversations they take part in. Blocked users cannot contact one another.
