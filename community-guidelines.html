Our shared values

Community Guidelines

TUDO works because people show up as their real, respectful selves. These are the promises we make to each other.

✅ Be real

Use your own recent photos and honest information. No fake profiles or impersonation.

💛 Be kind

Lead with respect. Harassment, hate speech and threats have no place here.

🔒 Respect privacy

Never share someone's private details or photos without their clear consent.

🛡️ Keep it safe

No scams, no money requests, no moving people off-platform to pressure them.

🔞 Adults only

TUDO is strictly 18+. Report any account you believe belongs to a minor.

🚫 No explicit content

Keep photos and messages appropriate. Suspected explicit images may be blurred until reviewed.

When guidelines are broken

Depending on severity we may warn, temporarily suspend, or permanently ban an account. Serious cases get human review, and members can appeal. Safety-critical reports are prioritised.
